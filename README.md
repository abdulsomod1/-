# 📚 معهد أصول اللغة العربية الإسلامي — Digital Research Library

> **العلم والإيمان والتقوى**  
> **Knowledge, Faith & Piety**

A modern Arabic digital research library for **معهد أصول اللغة العربية الإسلامي (MAHID Foundation for Arabic & Islamic Studies), Ibadan, Nigeria**.

The platform is designed to provide a central digital archive where the institute's students' academic research papers can be viewed, organized, searched, and downloaded as PDF documents.

---

## 🏫 About the Project

The **Islamic Arabic Language Foundation / معهد أصول اللغة العربية الإسلامي** Digital Research Library is being developed as an online academic archive for the institute's research papers.

The platform will allow visitors to:

- Browse academic research papers
- Search for research by title or researcher
- Browse research by category
- Browse research by academic year
- View researcher information
- View graduating classes
- Read research information
- Download research papers as PDF
- Share individual research pages

The current frontend uses **demo research data and placeholder files**.

Real research covers, PDFs, student images, and final academic information will be added later.
